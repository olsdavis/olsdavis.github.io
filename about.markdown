I'm Oscar Davis, a PhD student in Machine Learning at the University of Oxford, working primarily on generative modelling.

My research interests include flow matching, diffusion models, generative modelling on manifolds, and discrete generative models. I am supervised by [Prof Michael Bronstein](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/michael.bronstein/), [Dr İsmail Ceylan](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/ismaililkan.ceylan/), and [Dr Joey Bose](https://joeybose.github.io/).
